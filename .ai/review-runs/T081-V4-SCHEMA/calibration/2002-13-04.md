## Obligations and branches

1. `req-replace-magneto`: one-time replacement within 10 flight hours after effective date (`ev-deadline`, `ev-replace-range`).
2. `req-check-removed-magneto`: inspect removed magneto for stop pin; if missing, select exactly one engine-family branch (`ev-inspection-branch-a`, `ev-inspection-branch-b`).
3. `req-installation-prohibition`: after effective date, prohibit installation of listed model/range magnetos on any engine (`ev-prohibition`).

Replacement with a serial outside the affected range ends the immediate
replacement obligation, subject to the installation prohibition. Bulletin
00-6A is identified but not retained and supplies only a part-number
cross-reference; unseen bulletin content supplies no additional fact.
