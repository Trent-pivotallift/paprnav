## Applicability semantics

- Engine product scope: the exact Lycoming and TCM model enumerations in `ev-product-scope`.
- Installed-part condition: crankshaft repaired by Nelson Balancing Service under certificate NB7R820J during the inclusive date period.
- Table 1 is evidence to determine work-order/repair identity, not a substitute for the broader source rule: the preamble and paragraph (a) say Nelson records are incomplete.
- If engine/crankshaft was not repaired during the period or was not repaired by Nelson -> no further action.
- If repairer cannot be determined -> the rule expressly requires compliance; preserve provenance as unknown while activating the source-mandated fail-safe branch.

## Engine-logbook and installation relationship

Table 1 work orders, repair dates, engine serials, return-to-service tags, and
crankshaft-flange markings are engine/crankshaft evidence. They remain attached
to the engine/component logbook record, not copied into a generic airframe
logbook record as independent authority.

The aircraft record links to the engine through a time-bounded installation
relationship. Aircraft-ID evaluation must inspect the relevant current or
historical installed engine, then compare its engine model, work order, repair
date, engine serial where known, return-to-service evidence, and crankshaft
marking with `ev-product-scope`, `ev-work-order-table`, and `ev-determine`.
An engine transfer between aircraft does not sever or recreate its evidence
history. A blank Table 1 engine serial remains unknown and is not an exclusion.

## Obligations, timing, and branches

1. `req-determine-applicability`: within 10 hours TIS after effective date, evaluate repair history and evidence (`ev-determine`).
2. `req-inspect-or-remove`: for affected or repairer-unknown cases, within the same 10-hour window choose either the three-part inspection path or removal/replacement (`ev-inspect-or-remove`).
3. `req-replace-or-rework`: conditional on any failed inspection/check, replace unless qualifying rework brings the crankshaft within approved limits (`ev-replace-or-rework`).
4. `req-related-ad-check`: for removed TCM IO-360/IO-520/TSIO-520 series crankshafts, preserve the AD 97-26-17 cross-reference; it is not satisfaction of this AD (`ev-related-ad`).

No service bulletin is incorporated. Overhaul/maintenance manuals and
FAA-approved repair-station data are approved-data references whose exact
identity is unknown in this retained rule.
