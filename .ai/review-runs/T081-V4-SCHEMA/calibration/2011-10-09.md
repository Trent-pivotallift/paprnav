## Obligations and timing

`ev-cycle` is one shared recurring timing group for the ten ordered actions.
The initial terms use different anchors but one `whichever-first` group; the
recurring terms use last compliance and `whichever-first`.

Each recurring completion annotation must identify the completed action set,
completion date, and aircraft TIS. The next bounds are 100 hours TIS and 12
months after that qualifying completion, whichever occurs first. A partial
entry that does not establish completion of every active action in the shared
group cannot reset the whole recurring interval.

1. `req-g1`: inspect rails for dirt/debris; remove any found before further flight (`ev-g1-g3`).
2. `req-g2`: remove the seat using the ordered substeps (`ev-g1-g3`).
3. `req-g3`: inspect locking-pin holes; replace rail at 0.42 inch or more before flight; replacement does not terminate recurrence (`ev-g1-g3`, `ev-g3-nonterm-g5`).
4. `req-g4`: inspect rollers/washers; replace flat/worn parts or clean binding before flight; do not lubricate (`ev-g3-nonterm-g5`).
5. `req-g5`: inspect tang thickness; replace housing below 0.05 inch before flight; non-terminating (`ev-g3-nonterm-g5`).
6. `req-g6`: inspect tang length; replace housing below 0.230 inch before flight; non-terminating (`ev-g6-g8`).
7. `req-g7`: inspect lock-pin springs and replace deficient springs before flight (`ev-g6-g8`).
8. `req-g8`: inspect rails for cracks and replace when Figure 5 criteria are exceeded before flight; non-terminating (`ev-g6-g8`).
9. `req-g9`: reinstall the seat in the stated sequence (`ev-g9-g10`).
10. `req-g10`: inspect pin engagement; repair/replace below 0.15 inch before flight; non-terminating (`ev-g9-g10`).

Figures 1-5 are part of the retained source and must remain available to the
reviewer; their dimensional meaning must not be reconstructed from OCR alone.
No service bulletin is incorporated. The AMOC carryover is AD-level authority,
not evidence that an aircraft used an AMOC.
